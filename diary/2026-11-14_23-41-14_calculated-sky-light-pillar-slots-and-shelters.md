## Completed

Four steps of the plan, in one working tree.

1. **256 slots in a uniform buffer.** `LightPillarSelection.SLOT_COUNT` is `256`. The four uniform arrays of `48` are replaced by the std140 block `PillarSlots { vec4 pillarSlots[768]; }`, three `vec4` per drawn pillar, `12 KB`. `fillPillars` packs only the slots that show, in slot order, and `drawRibbons` draws only their ribbons, a prefix of the ribbon buffer's indices through `RenderSystem.drawElements` with the type of the shared sequential index buffer. The buffer is orphaned with `glBufferData` and filled with `glBufferSubData` each frame, bound at point `8`, and the generic binding and point `8`'s binding and range are restored after. The glitter hash takes `slot * 2 + kind` (`pillarSeed`), since the draw index changes as other pillars come and go. The cell atlas has `atlasBands` bands (a uniform now), grown by `LightPillarCells.grownBands` in steps of `2` up to `16` within `GL_MAX_TEXTURE_SIZE`; pillars beyond its bands compute their cells per pixel. The selection finds a key's slot through a `Long2IntOpenHashMap` sized for `512`, so it never rehashes, instead of scanning `256` slots for each of `256` chosen keys.
2. **The linear sum.** Unless the fill view or debug mode `4` is on, the screen pass writes linear light times the slot's opacity (fade times fog) into an `RGBA16F` target the size of the main target, added with `GL_ONE, GL_ONE` and cleared each frame. A full screen pass, `light_pillars_composite.fsh` over `FHShaders.IMAGE_V`, writes `encodeSrgb(1 - exp(-sum)) * opacity` with the old screen blend, where the opacity is the daylight and rain share, and discards where the sum is `0`. Overlaps now show the tone curve of their summed light instead of up to `1.29` times too bright. The fill view and debug mode `4` draw as before.
3. **Option C, walls per light.** `LightPillarShelter` is now a reader for one light. `LightPillarSources.walk` reads every light's cap, `32` rims, and `32` ceilings, and `LightPillarClusters` merges touching lights only when their caps are equal and every rim and ceiling rounds to the same step of `2` degrees (`LightPillarShelter.comparedStep`, angles clamped to `0` to `90` degrees). A cluster keeps the shelter of its shelter light, and the selection copies it into the slot each tick, so there is no per-slot reading, tracking, or ready wait any more: new slots fade in at once. A rim box (the extent widened by `RIM_REACH`, from the lowest flame level to the highest block read) joins the cap box, `queueSectionsReadingShelterAt` scans the chunk columns within `RIM_REACH` of a changed block, and a column that gets its light queues the sections of the `8` columns around it whose rim boxes reach into it (`queueSectionsReadingSheltersIn`). `/calculatedSky pillars` loses the shelters line, and the world changes line counts sections "for their shelters". While a section is walked, `LightPillarShelter.beginSection` keeps each column's wall top and overhang bottom by flame level and highest block read, so a floor of lights at one level reads each column once instead of up to `24` times.
4. **Ceilings for overhangs.** The same column read finds, above the wall or above the flame's level, the bottom of the first block that stops light, up to `WALL_HEIGHT` above the light's top. Each direction's ceiling is the lowest `atan2(bottom - top, exit)` over the overhang columns its level ray crosses, with `exit` where it leaves the column's square, or `OPEN_CEILING` (`90` degrees). `LightPillarOptics.shelteredCap` folds the ceiling toward the eye into the cap relative to the eye as `d tan(c)`, so the shader is unchanged there. Light never passes above an overhang.

## Decisions

- One working tree for all four steps, since empireu asked for as much as possible while away. Steps 1 and 3 overlap: step 1's shelter build budget was left out, because step 3 removes the per-slot builds it was for.
- The atlas grows by `2` bands at a time. `RGBA16F` for the pillar light, `16.6 MB` at `1080p`; `R11F_G11F_B10F` would halve that with `6` and `5` bit mantissas.
- Rims compare as `0` below the horizon, ceilings as open near the vertical. The cluster keeps its shelter light's exact angles, not the rounded ones.
- A change in a light's own column above its cap but inside its rim box now queues its section too. This rewalks one section more than needed and keeps the boxes simple.
- No before run. Everything before this change is committed in `60a159a58`, so a before run can be taken from that commit.
- A chunk unload does not queue the sections around it. Their lights keep the walls they read while it was loaded, which are still there, where a fresh read would see open air. The chunk's next load queues them through `queueSectionsReadingSheltersIn`. The old per-slot shelter re-read them on unload.
- The atlas at the default `0.25` degrees has `420` rows, so all `16` bands take `35 MB`. It grows at most `8` times and never shrinks while the cell size stays.
