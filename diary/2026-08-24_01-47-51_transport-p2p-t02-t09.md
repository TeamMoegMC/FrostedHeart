## Decisions

- `TeamTownData` is the sole connection authority; terminal block entities own only local device state and runtime work.
- One directed binding creates one sender-owned reservation. Bidirectional pairing is one connection ID containing two directed bindings.
- Sender and receiver redstone facts are persisted separately. Their logical OR pauses a direction and releases its reservation; resumption
  restores the accepted reservation even when town capacity has since fallen.
- Binding an already powered endpoint participates in admission as paused rather than being incorrectly rejected at its nominal rate.
- Guarantees cover complete server calls, normal save/stop, and chunk lifecycle. No hard-crash recovery journal was added.
- Binding, filter summary, and transport snapshot limits remain independently validated at 4096 entries.
- The snapshot limit applies to the combined warehouse and P2P reservation list; new bindings cannot overflow that total.
