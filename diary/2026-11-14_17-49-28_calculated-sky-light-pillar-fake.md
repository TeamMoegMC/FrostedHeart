## Completed

Light pillars are drawn as a fake instead of the integral along the view ray, following `D:\repos\TWR\PILLAR_FAKE.md` and the fixes of `D:\repos\TWR\scratch\pillar-fake-fix-prompt.md` and `D:\repos\TWR\scratch\pillar-fake-fix-prompt-2.md`, after `2026-11-14_15-50-53_calculated-sky-light-pillar-cells.md`.

- `LightPillarClusters` merges only lights that touch, replacing the `8` by `8` by `8` cells of the earlier entries:
  - Per chunk section, a union-find joins each light to its `13` earlier neighbors of the `26` around it, in the scan order `y`, `z`, `x`. A cluster's root is its light with the lowest block index, and its key is `BlockPos.asLong` of that light, so a rescan in any order keeps the keys. There is no size limit, and a cluster only splits at a section boundary.
  - Full blocks and liquids span their block across, other lights are points at their block's center. A cluster stands at the middle of its extent across `x` and `z` and at its strength-weighted height, and carries its half extents in `x` and `z` and its light count.
  - Its shelter is read at the light with the highest top nearest the middle of the extent, lower block index first on ties, and that light's full block flag is the cluster's. `LightPillarOccluders.isFullBlock` tests `isCollisionShapeFullBlock` and counts a failing block as not full.
  - Each section's clusters are a doubly linked list over the cluster arrays, and removing a cluster moves the last one into its place and fixes its neighbors and the section's head. `LightPillarSelection.isWithinChunkRadius` reads the chunk from the block key, since `CELL_SIZE` is gone.
- `LightPillarShelter` replaces `LightPillarHorizons` for drawing. Per slot it reads a cap and `32` rim directions from the center of the shelter light's block:
  - The cap is the bottom of the lowest block that stops light from `ceil(top)` up to `WORLD_SURFACE` in the light's own column. Only a full block whose own cap is `ceil(top)`, so whose top is covered, uses the highest of its `4` side columns' caps instead, or open where one of them is open. A torch under a porch, a 3 by 3 roof, or an inverted U is capped by its own column, a torch on a bare wall stays open, and a lamp set into a wall is open through the column in front of it.
  - A column within `2` blocks has a wall where the block at `floor(top)` stops light, up to the top of the blocks that stop light from there, read up to `top + 16`. Direction `i` is centered at `(i + 0.5) * 11.25 degrees` from `+x` toward `+z`. Its ray meets each wall column's square, widened by `1e-4`, in a 2D slab test, and its angle is the steepest `atan2(wallTop - top, max(entry, 0.05))` over the squares it enters, or `OPEN_RIM = -PI / 2` where it enters none.
  - `rimToward(slot, eyeX, eyeZ)` blends the two directions nearest to the eye's azimuth linearly, each counted as at least `0`, so a pillar is lifted only by the walls between the light and the eye.
  - It reuses `LightPillarHorizons.Columns`, `LevelColumns`, and `LightPillarOccluders`, follows the slots as the horizons did, and marks a slot ready once built. Block changes reach it with their height through `LightPillarWorldChanges`, and chunk loads and unloads by chunk. A slot is rebuilt for a change at or above `floor(top)` in its own column, and for a full block also in its side columns, from `floor(top)` up to `floor(top) + 16` in the other columns, in one of its chunks, or when it takes a new key, moves, or changes its full block flag. Fixed arrays, no allocation in the tick.
- `LightPillarHorizons` is no longer updated, hooked, or uploaded. The class and its GameTests stay.
- `light_pillars.glsl` computes the closed form at level tilt: `R(e) / (2 d) * G(psi)` with `s^2 = lateralSpread^2 + b^2`, times the dust density at the mirror height and `exp(-optical depth)` along both legs. Both legs are straight at the angle `e`, so their optical depth is `extinction * (2 C(z) - C(z_S) - C(0)) / sin e` with `C` the dust column. The glitter count is `2 a_f r_1^2 r_2^2 G / (d max(sin e, 0.02)^2)`.
  - The pillar starts at `max(|beta_S|, rim)` and fades in from there over `pillarFadeInSigmas` tilt sigmas as `ramp * mix(pillarBaseShare, 1, ramp)` with `ramp` a smoothstep, so a pillar lifted by walls fades in like any other.
  - It shows below the cap on the sheet at `h = d tan e`, softened over `0.5` blocks.
  - A cluster's pillar is widened across by `a = halfWidthAcross / d`, with `halfWidthAcross = (|hx oz| + |hz ox|) / |o|` from its half extents and its horizontal offset `o` from the eye, set per frame in `spans[48]`. For `a <= 0.5 s` the lateral profile is a Gaussian of variance `s^2 + a^2 / 3`, the old one at `a = 0`, and above it the Gaussian smeared over `[-a, a]`, `(Q((|psi| - a) / s) - Q((|psi| + a) / s)) / (2 a)`, with `Q` from the erfc of Abramowitz and Stegun 7.1.26. Both integrate to `1`, so a cluster carries the light of all its lights. The glitter's mean count is times the light count, so merged glints keep their density. Ribbon rows are `a + spreads * s + margin` wide, and the count branch of their width needs a positive center.
  - Near the eye the value fades in by `smoothstep(0.25 nearFade, nearFade, eyeLeg)` with `nearFade = pillarNearFade` (default `8`) and `eyeLeg` the length of the leg from the eye to the crystal.
  - The fade in, the cap, and the near fade weigh the value only. The glitter's mean count is left alone, so glints keep their density and dim.
  - The fragment stage hides the pillar by `smoothstep(d - band, d + band, P.xz . u)` with `band = pillarDepthBand`, and shows it fully for sky and for scene points at or above the cloud height.
  - Ribbon rows run from the same lowest elevation to `min(89.5 degrees, atan(cap / d))`, with widths from the closed form's spread. The cell origin stays `max(beta_S, -beta_S - 6 sigma)` (`CELL_ORIGIN_SIGMAS`, in the shader and in `LightPillarCells`), so the shelter changes cell values, not cell positions.
  - The integral, `tiltSum`, `sceneShare`, `tiltMass`, `nearTiltMass`, `normalShare`, and the reappearance band are gone from the shader.
- `LightPillarRenderer` uploads `shelters[48]` in place of `overhangs[48]`, the rim toward the eye in radians and the cap relative to the eye (`1e6` when open), and `spans[48]`, the half width across in blocks and the light count, `(0, 1)` in unused slots. ## Decisions

- The rim, the cap, and the fade in are in the value function, so with cells they apply at the cell's center through the atlas, and the vertex stage's widths follow them too.
- The scene test runs before the value, so pixels whose scene is in front of the light are discarded without computing anything.
- The base factor of section 3.2 is the setting `pillarBaseShare`, default `0.5` as the plan suggests.
- `LightPillarShelter` builds every marked slot in the tick that marks it, without a budget. A build reads at most `24` columns of `17` blocks and the five cap columns up to the surface.
- Caps are stored as world heights with `OPEN_CAP` infinite, and the renderer turns an open cap into the finite stand-in.
- First round, changed after review: the fade weight also thinned the glitter count, which looked wrong in game, and the rim was a height on the sheet, which lifted nothing for a light below the eye (empireu, in game: every pillar started at the same height whatever the walls). The cap read only the light's own column, so a lamp in a wall had none. The first round also renamed three calls in `LightPillarWorldChanges` and `CalculatedSkyClientCommand` with `sed`, against the rules. Their result stays as it is, and this round changed files only with the edit tools.
- Third round, after empireu's second look in game: the rim ignored walls in the `4` side columns, a pillar showed above an inverted U of planks, two torches `2` or `3` blocks apart merged into one pillar between them, and pillars did not dim near the eye. empireu asked to stop the cells and merge only lights that touch, drawn as a larger pillar.
  - The rim of `PILLAR_FAKE.md` lifts a beam only inside an enclosure and leaves it for one wall or a corner. empireu chose the rim toward the eye instead: for level plates the pillar at elevation `e` is light that left the light at `e` toward the eye, so walls lift a pillar only on their side. `D:\repos\TWR\scratch\pillar-fake-round3-design.md` holds the reasoning.
  - The prompt's `8` by `8` by `8` limit on a cluster is dropped, since it would split a 3 by 3 floor at `x` `7` to `9`. Clusters still split at section boundaries.
  - The prompt would have every light span half a block, `a = 0.5 / d`. At `20` blocks that is `1.43 degrees` against `0.27 degrees` of spread for plates, which would widen every torch's pillar several times, so lights that are not full blocks or liquids are points.
  - The prompt left the glitter count per cluster, which would make the glints of merged lights `N` times sparser and brighter, so the count is times the light count.
  - The cap of a full block uses its side columns only when its top is covered. Otherwise a glowstone under a beam one block wide showed its pillar through the beam, from the side columns beside it.
  - The shelter is read at a light's block center, not at the cluster's middle, which can fall between blocks or on an empty column, where every ray of the rim starts on a column's edge.
- Limits kept, found in review:
  - A wide cluster's walls and cap are read at one light, so a pillar over a floor is lifted and capped as at its middle light.
  - One rim and one cap serve a whole ribbon, which is wide for a wide cluster.
  - At the diagonals the `32` directions over-cut a wall's angle by about `2 degrees`, between two directions.
  - Every light that does not touch another is its own cluster, so the `48` slots fill sooner in dense places and `LightPillarSelection.choose` has more clusters to rank each tick.
  - The cell atlas holds `16` cells of `0.25 degrees` on each side of a plate pillar, `4 degrees`, and `16 degrees` for wobbling plates. A wider pillar, such as a 3 by 3 glowstone floor at `20` blocks with `a = 4.3 degrees`, is drawn per pixel.
  - A column in a chunk that is not loaded reads as open, so a light at the edge of the loaded area shows its pillar without that column's cap or walls until the chunk loads and rebuilds it.
  - `halfWidthAt` in the shader names `LightPillarOptics.ribbonHalfWidth` as its twin, but that twin still follows the integral. The closed form's ribbon width has no Java twin, and only its bound on the widened profile is tested.
  - A rim direction below the horizon takes the farthest wall it enters, since that is the steepest. `rimToward` counts every direction as at least `0`, so this never shows.
